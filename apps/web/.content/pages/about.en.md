# About SoiGuru

SoiGuru lists restaurants, cafés, massage, tours, scooter rental, nail salons, dive shops and hotels in Krabi, Chiang Mai and Lam Plai Mat. Each listing says who last updated it and when. Owners can claim their listing to keep their hours, photos and contact details correct.
